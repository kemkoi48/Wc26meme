Before you touch a ticker

  1. Relative volume, not the % gain. A big move on normal volume is thin. Check the volume column before the price column.
  2. Name the catalyst in one sentence. Not "it's green" — why. If you can't say it out loud, you don't know whether the crowd is buying or selling into you.
  3. Early, or already extended? Pull it up in the chart on the right before entering. Price fading off its high means the move is done.
  4. Decide the exit before you enter. Not after it goes against you.
