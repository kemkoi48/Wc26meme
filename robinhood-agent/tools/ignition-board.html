Reading the chart — candlestick basics (so "how did it qualify" isn't a guess)

Every 5-minute candle on this page's charts is built the same way real trading platforms draw one — this isn't a house style, it's the standard convention Warrior Trading, TradingView, and every broker chart use. Once you can read a candle, the "Chart" column on the table below and the cards above stop being decoration and start telling you how a stock got here, not just that it did.

Wick (the thin line): the full high-to-low range traded during that candle's time window.
Body (the thick block): the range between the open and the close.
Green/up body: close finished above the open — buyers won that window.
Red/down body: close finished below the open — sellers won that window.
Long wick, small body: price got rejected — it traveled far, then got pushed back near where it started.
Doji
Open ≈ close, long wicks both ways. Indecision — buyers and sellers fought to a draw. Often marks a pause before the next real move.
Hammer
Small body near the top, long lower wick. Sellers pushed price way down, buyers dragged it back up. Bullish, especially after a decline.
Shooting star
Small body near the bottom, long upper wick. Buyers pushed price way up, sellers slammed it back down. Bearish, especially after a run-up — a classic sign a "qualified" mover is about to fade.
Bullish engulfing
A small red body immediately followed by a bigger green body that fully swallows it. Buyers overwhelmed the prior sellers in one candle — a real reversal-up signal, not just noise.
Bearish engulfing
A small green body immediately followed by a bigger red body that fully swallows it. Sellers overwhelmed the prior buyers in one candle — worth respecting on a row that already qualified, it can mean the move is over.
How to actually use this here: a row qualifying (4/4 pillars) tells you the numbers lined up at that instant — the "Qualified" timestamp says when. The chart next to it tells you whether that instant looks like buyers still in control (green bodies, short upper wicks, each low higher than the last) or already fading (shooting stars, bearish engulfing, long upper wicks into the high). Warrior Trading's own scanner shows this exact combination — a live number plus a live chart next to it — because the number alone can't tell you if you're catching the move or catching the top of it.
