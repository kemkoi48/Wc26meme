Warrior Trading's 5 pillars — what "Pillars" and "Order Book" actually check
  1. Relative volume ≥ 5x. A big move on normal volume is thin — maybe a couple of prints. This is a real numeric pillar, scored automatically.
  2. % change ≥ 10%. Also scored automatically, from the scan's own number.
  3. Price $2–$20. Scored automatically from the Last column.
  4. Float ≤ 20M. Scored automatically — but only on the two scans that report float (Early Momentum Ignition, Warrior Trading Style). The Pillars column shows "n/a" for this one on scans that don't carry it, rather than silently failing it.
  5. Catalyst — the 5th pillar, and it is NOT numeric. Nothing here can automate it. Can you name the reason in one sentence? If you can't say it out loud, you're guessing what the crowd is doing and don't know if they're buying or selling into you. This is the one pillar you always have to check yourself.
Order Book (click "Check" on any row) shows real resting Level-2 depth — how many shares are waiting to buy vs. sell in the top 10 price levels right now. This is limit-order pressure, not executed trade flow — Robinhood's data doesn't classify individual trades as buyer- or seller-initiated, so this is a real but partial picture of who wants to trade, not who already did.
